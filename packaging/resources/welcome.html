RenkySum @VERSION@

A per-channel vintage console summing plug-in: three console models (CLASS-A XFMR, VCA BUS, DISCRETE OP-AMP), adjustable harmonics, a 3D Depth control, and per-instance component tolerances keyed to a Channel number, so every track gets its own subtly different strip. CHANNEL instances encode with sin(), one MIX BUS instance on the master decodes the sum with asin() (airwindows-Console style).

On the next screen tick the formats you want (Audio Unit, VST3, standalone app). Plug-ins go to /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins; the app goes to /Applications. Rescan plug-ins in your DAW afterwards; it appears under Renky Denk > RenkySum.

Requires macOS 11 or later, Apple Silicon or Intel.
