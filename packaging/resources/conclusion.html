Installed

Rescan plug-ins in your DAW (Logic: it scans on launch; Live: Preferences > Plug-Ins > Rescan; Reaper: Preferences > Plug-ins > Re-scan). Look under Renky Denk > RenkySum.

Suggested workflow: one instance per track with Stage = CHANNEL and a different Channel number on each, plus one instance on the master with Stage = MIX BUS. Turn on Auto Gain for loudness-honest A/B comparisons.
