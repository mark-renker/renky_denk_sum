RenkySum - manual macOS install (if you prefer not to use the .pkg)

1. Copy "RenkySum.component" to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/
2. Copy "RenkySum.vst3" to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
3. Optionally copy "RenkySum.app" (standalone) to /Applications
4. Because the bundles were downloaded (not installed by a .pkg), macOS quarantines them. Clear that once:
      xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/RenkySum.component \
                                     ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/RenkySum.vst3
5. Rescan plug-ins in your DAW. The plug-in appears under "Renky Denk".
